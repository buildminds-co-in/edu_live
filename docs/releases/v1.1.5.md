## Changes
- Student app: the 60-mark option is removed from Total Marks for question papers; the options are 20, 25, 30, 40 and 50 (70 was already removed in v1.1.1). (#15)
- Why: Maths 60-mark papers fail with "the AI model's response came back incomplete or malformed". Prod logs show the cause: the generation output budget is 600 tokens per question (`TOKENS_PER_QUESTION` in `questionPaperGeneration.ts`), so 16,800 for a 28-question Maths 60 paper; the failed attempt used 16,738 and was cut off. Maths 50 succeeded at 10,924 of 12,600 (87%, so it can occasionally fail too); Maths 40 used 6,500 of 10,200.
- The worker still accepts any marks total from 1 to 500 (unchanged); only the dropdown changed.
