## Changes
- Question-paper and answers PDFs: maths renders properly (superscripts, fractions, symbols). `$…$` is converted to MathML in the Worker with `katex` 0.16.9 (new worker dependency). Before, PDFs printed raw LaTeX such as `$2^3 \times 3^a$`. (#<PR number>)
- The duplicate raw-code formula box under questions (e.g. `2^3 \times 3^a \times b \times 7`, `\tan \theta`) is gone, in the app and the PDFs.
- PDFs rendered by the old code are not served again; they are re-rendered on the next download (`PDF_RENDER_VERSION`).
- Page images are cached for one hour instead of a year, and the app adds a version to image links, so the blank Maths screenshot no longer sticks in browsers.
- Answers read like proper maths working, in the app and the PDFs (fixes answers already saved too, because the text is cleaned at display time): answer keys came back with line breaks written as the two characters `\` `n` (169 in one prod Maths key, no real breaks), so the working ran together and KaTeX showed red `\n`. Now: real line breaks (LaTeX commands like `\neq` kept); inline maths the model left open before a line break is closed (this swallowed words like "Rectification and correct solution" into maths); common LaTeX written outside `$…$` becomes a symbol (`34 m \times 24 m` → 34 m × 24 m, `\alpha` → α); common mojibake repaired (`mÂ²` → m²); fractions render full size (`\frac` as `\dfrac`). Scan over every saved paper (prod 7, staging 16; 962 questions and answers): `\n` as text 370 → 2 (both `\neq`/`\ne`), maths that fails to render 21 → 0, nothing made worse.
- Student app, Q. Paper tab: buttons "Generate Question Paper", "Download Question Paper", "Generate Answers", "Download Answers"; answers grouped by section with numbers matching the paper; the answers PDF is titled "<Subject> Answers (<n> Marks)".
- Layout: the Q. Paper panel is one scroll area using the full height (widget, phone, desktop), wider on large desktop screens. Fixed a bug where maths made the page taller than the screen (an extra scrollbar and an empty grey band), in Q. Paper and Chat.
